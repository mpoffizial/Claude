KEY INSIGHTS:
=============
1. LONG-ONLY is critical for gold (structural bullish bias)
   - Long+Short: Most strategies lost money
   - Long-only: Consistent profits

2. WIDER STOPS prevent premature exits
   - 1.5x ATR SL → more stops hit
   - 2.5x ATR SL → optimal: gives trades room to breathe

3. LET WINNERS RUN
   - TP at 6.0x ATR outperformed 3.0x and 4.0x
   - Trailing stop captures profits on big moves

4. CONFLUENCE FILTERING is the key
   - Single indicator strategies: inconsistent
   - 5+ indicators agreeing: profitable and lower drawdown
